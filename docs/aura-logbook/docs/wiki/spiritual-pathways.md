## Pathways

Aura Logbook lets you tailor your experience by choosing a pathway that matches your style: Mindful Living, Energy (Astrology & Chakras), or Christian Faith. All pathways include the same core mood logging and dashboard features, but each offers unique ways to reflect and grow.

## Unique and Shared Features

| Pathway | Description | Unique features | Shared features (all users) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mindful Living | Calm, clarity, and self-awareness. No spiritual content—just mindful tips and journaling. | Mindful journaling, meditation tips, science-based stats, cognitive prompts | Mood logging, dashboard, weekly stats, aura color selection |
| Energy (Astrology & Chakras) | Explore moods and growth through star signs, chakras, and your own energy. | Zodiac overlays, birth chart widget, chakra wheel, energy color insights, spiritual affirmations | Same as above |
| Christian Faith | A journey guided by scripture, prayer, and Christian gratitude. | Bible verse of the day, scripture-linked prompts, prayer and gratitude journaling, affirmation reminders | Same as above |

## Shared Features

Regardless of their pathway(s), all users get:

- **Mood tracking:** Add/view moods by date and color.
- **Journaling:** Daily log/reflection area.
- **Dashboard:** Overview with mood trends, recent entries, and basic stats.
- **Aura color selection:** Pick/display a color to represent their current mood/energy.
- **Profile:** Basic info, avatar, pathway selection, motto, etc.
- **Weekly summary:** Recent streaks, breakdowns.
- **Optional:** Weather card, daily stat widgets.
