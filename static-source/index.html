03 / How I work

Make it clear.
Then make it better.

Good systems should support real people in real-world conditions.

  1. 01

    Understand the friction

    Map the current workflow, clarify requirements, and find where people or information get stuck.

  2. 02

    Build the right structure

    Choose a maintainable approach, define the data and components, and implement with purpose.

  3. 03

    Test, document, refine

    Check behavior, make the process repeatable, and automate where it reduces real work.
