What I designed

Assessed how a small business was actually running day to day — scattered files, shared logins in emails, tasks living in someone's head — and designed a system to replace that. Built a Notion hub covering account access, shared OneDrive file storage, task and project tracking, documentation, and content planning. The structure follows the business's real workflows, not a generic template.

Built to hand off

The whole point is that the system works without me. QR signage on-site links staff directly to the guides they need. Plain-language documentation explains the why behind each section, not just what to click. Anyone joining the team can get oriented without a walkthrough from the owner.

What this demonstrates

Independent client work: requirements gathering, workflow analysis, system design, and delivery. The same skills that apply inside a development team — understanding what someone actually needs, designing for the person who comes next — applied to a small-business context.

Proof-of-work next step: a permission-cleared client example with the starting workflow, deliverables, screenshots, and a verified outcome.
