Hair Log

A structured Shortcut workflow for remembering hair-care decisions, products, dates, and results.

Independent workApple Shortcuts · Structured records · Personal analytics

On this page

The friction

Hair-care decisions are easy to lose track of: which product was used, when a style was installed, what changed, and whether the result was worth repeating. Reconstructing that history later makes comparison harder.

The technical solution

I designed a Shortcut-based logging workflow that captures a repeatable record at the moment of care. The system turns quick prompts into structured entries that can be revisited instead of relying on memory or scattered notes.

  1. Collect the date and care event.
  2. Capture products, notes, and relevant observations.
  3. Store the result in a consistent, searchable format.
  4. Use prior entries as reference for future decisions.

Evidence & current limits

The project is grounded in the Shortcut workflow and intended record structure. Public screenshots, export files, and measured long-term outcomes have not yet been attached.

Next proof-of-work step: publish a redacted sample entry and a short walkthrough using non-sensitive example data. This page describes the design and intended benefit; it does not claim measured results.
